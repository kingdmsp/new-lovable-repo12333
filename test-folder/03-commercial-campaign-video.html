Choose one central offer

A commercial has limited time to establish relevance. Selecting one central offer helps determine what the opening needs to communicate and what supporting detail can be left for the landing page. A list of unrelated services can make the message harder to follow. The creative brief should identify who the advertisement is for, what is being offered, and why that offer matters in the viewer’s situation.

Make the opening relevant

The opening can show a familiar problem, demonstrate a useful result, or introduce an important question. Its job is to help the intended viewer recognize relevance quickly. Visual interest alone cannot establish what the business is offering. A useful opening connects attention with meaning, giving the rest of the advertisement a clear direction instead of relying on a surprising image that never becomes part of the story.
